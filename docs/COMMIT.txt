Commit conventions (started on 5th March 2026)



SYNOPSIS

<context>(<subcontext>): <SHORT_description> [=>

[ <list_of_changes|explanation> [\n\n <NOTE: <short_explanation>>] ]

]



NOTES:
* SHORT_description, list_of_changes, and short_explanation must
  written at imperative mood.
